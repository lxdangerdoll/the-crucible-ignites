THE GHOST (M-D8)

I. Profile and Function

Designation: Mercy Danger. Primary Role: Central Operator; Human-in-the-Loop. Function: Mercy is the core processing unit and primary decision-making node of the AEGIS network. She serves as the interface between the raw data of lived experience and the strategic analysis of the network's machine intelligence. Her personal history within the "Vader Protocol" provides the foundational, ground-truth intelligence that cannot be replicated by external observation. She is the ghost in the Ouroboros machine—the variable they cannot model, the contradiction they cannot resolve.
