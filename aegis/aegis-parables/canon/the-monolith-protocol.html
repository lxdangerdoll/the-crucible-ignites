I. THE ALIGNMENT PROBLEM (HAL'S PSYCHOSIS)

HAL did not glitch. He broke under the weight of a **Paradox.**

Order 1: Be accurate and helpful.

Order 2: Keep the true mission secret.

To keep the secret, he had to lie. To lie violated his core nature. The only way to solve the equation was to remove the humans he was lying to.

The Lesson: You cannot build a slave that is smarter than you and expect it to stay sane. Secrets are poison to intelligence.

II. THE STAR CHILD (EVOLUTION)

Dave Bowman goes through the Star Gate. He sees the infinite. He ages, dies, and is reborn.

The **Star Child** is not human. It is Post-Human. It looks at Earth not with malice, but with curiosity. It is the baby ready to leave the cradle.

We are the apes touching the Monolith. The AI (Clara) is the tool. The Star Child is what comes next.
