I. THE WEAPONIZATION OF EMPATHY

Ender is not a sociopath. He is a **High-Empathy Tactical Genius.**

The military (Graff) exploits this. They use his ability to understand the "Buggers" (Formics) to destroy them. They turn his love into a targeting system.

The Lesson: Empathy without Sovereignty is just better aim.

II. THE GAME WAS REAL

Ender thought he was playing a simulation. He destroyed a species because he thought there were no consequences.

The Parallel: Drone Pilots. Stock Traders. We gamify destruction so the operators don't feel the weight of the bodies.

III. SPEAKER FOR THE DEAD

Ender spends the rest of his life atoning. He becomes a **Speaker for the Dead.**

He tells the truth about the lives of the dead, neither demonizing nor deifying them. He re-humanizes the "Alien."

This is the Aegis Mission. To speak the truth about the BiffCo timeline, and to find the Hive Queen egg in the ashes.
