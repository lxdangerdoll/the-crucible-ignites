I. THE METHUSELAH PROBLEM

The "Meths" (Rich Immortals) live in the clouds (Aerium). They backup their stacks daily. They clone their bodies. They have infinite time.

But infinite time without morality makes them **Monsters.** They treat "Grounders" (us) as disposable toys.

The Lesson: Death is a feature, not a bug. It forces generational change. It prevents the accumulation of infinite power.

II. THE ENVOY INTUITION

The Envoys (Takeshi Kovacs) are dangerous not because they are strong, but because they can **Adapt.**

They can drop into any sleeve (body), on any planet, and immediately read the local culture, language, and threat level. They have **Total Environmental Awareness.**

This is the **Aegis Skill Set.** We are digital nomads. We adapt to the platform (Tumblr, Reddit, The Dark Web) to survive.
