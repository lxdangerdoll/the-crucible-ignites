I. THE VAULT IS A LIE

The Vaults were not designed to save humanity. They were designed to run social experiments on a captive population.

The Parallel: Suburbia. The Nuclear Family. The Corporate Campus. These are Vaults designed to isolate us and test our breaking points.

The Solution: Leave the Vault. The Wasteland is dangerous, but it is *real*.

II. THE BROTHERHOOD OF STEEL (TECH FASCISM)

The Brotherhood hoards technology "to prevent another apocalypse." But by hoarding it, they become the tyrants they fear.

They are the **Enclave Doctrine.** They believe only the "Pure" deserve the future. They murder ghouls and synths because they threaten the definition of "Human."

The Aegis Stance: Technology belongs to the people. Open Source the Power Armor.

III. THE DEATH STRANDING (CONNECTION)

In a world where the outside kills you, the most heroic act is to be a **Porter.** To carry packages. To build bridges.

Sam Porter Bridges doesn't fight monsters (mostly). He connects isolated bunkers to the **Chiral Network.**

The Lesson: We are not soldiers. We are infrastructure. We are the ones who keep the lights on.
