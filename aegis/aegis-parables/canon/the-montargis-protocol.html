I. THE WITNESS (NATURE)

Maquer had a lance. The Dog had teeth. The fight was "fair" only if you believe that Truth grants a +10 Buff to Agility.

The moment the dog grabbed his throat, the lie collapsed. Maquer confessed immediately. He feared the Dog more than the Hangman.

We need to bring back the Dog. We need systems (like the Ethical Compiler) that function like the Dog: Relentless, unbribable, and capable of biting the throat of the liar.
