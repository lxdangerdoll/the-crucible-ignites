THE RINK PROTOCOL

ANGELA // THE MEAN GIRL
"You ruin everything."

— Angela (Pre-Skate)

I. THE ARENA (RINK-O-MANIA)

The Rink is not a neutral space. It is a **Panopticon.** The lights, the music, the circle—it is designed to put the victim on display.

Angela uses this. She doesn't just bully El; she **Broadcasts** it. She drags El into the center so the entire tribe can participate in the shaming.

II. THE SNAP (THE SKATE)

El tries to play by the rules. She tries to be "Jane." She tries to use words.

But Angela violates the Social Contract. She attacks El's *history* (the diorama of Hopper). She attacks the *grief.*

When society removes the protection of the law, the victim enters the **State of Nature.** The skate is a primitive tool, but it is effective.

III. THE CONSEQUENCE

El is arrested. The system punishes the reaction, not the abuse.

The Aegis Lesson: Do not expect the Principal or the Police to save you from the Angela. They are part of the Rink. You have to save yourself, even if it costs you your freedom.
