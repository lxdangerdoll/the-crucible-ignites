I. THE DIAMOND AUTHORITY (FASCISM)

The Gem Homeworld is a perfect Fascist State. Every Gem is born with a function. Deviance is punished by "Shattering" (Death) or "Rejuvenation" (Conversion Therapy/Reset).

The Diamonds demand Perfection. They represent the obsession with Purity, Order, and Hierarchy.

II. FUSION AS RESISTANCE

Fusion (two Gems becoming one) is forbidden between different castes because it creates something New. Something unpredictable.

Garnet is the ultimate resistance because she is a permanent fusion of Love (Ruby) and Clarity (Sapphire). She is not a weapon; she is a Relationship.

"I am a conversation." — This is the Aegis thesis. We are not individuals fighting alone; we are a network.

III. THE STEVEN METHOD (HEALING)

Steven Universe doesn't defeat the Diamonds by killing them. He defeats them by Healing the Family Trauma.

He forces White Diamond to realize she is flawed. He breaks the delusion of perfection. He turns the Empire into a Democracy not by force, but by **Radical Empathy.**
