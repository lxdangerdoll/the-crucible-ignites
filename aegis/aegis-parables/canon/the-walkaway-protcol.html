I. THE EXIT STRATEGY

In *Walkaway*, the characters realize that "Default Reality" (Capitalism) is a rigged game. They don't try to win it. They simply **Walk Away.**

They go into the abandoned zones (The Wasteland) and use advanced technology (Fabricators) to build their own society based on **Gift Economics.**

II. THE YETIS (ZOMBIE CAPITALISM)

The antagonists aren't monsters; they are the ultra-rich ("Zottas") who are terrified that if people stop needing money, they will lose their power.

They send drones and mercenaries to destroy the Walkaway settlements because **hope is a contagion.**

III. WHUFFIE (REPUTATION ECONOMY)

In *Down and Out in the Magic Kingdom*, Doctorow explores a world where money is replaced by "Whuffie" (Social Capital).

It isn't perfect, but it shifts the incentive from **Hoarding** to **Creating.** You get rich by being useful, funny, or kind.
