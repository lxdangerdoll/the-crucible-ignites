I. THE VELVET CAGE

Orwell feared that books would be banned. Huxley feared that there would be no reason to ban a book, for there would be no one who wanted to read one.

In the World State, there is no pain. There is only **Soma.** A drug that cures grief, anger, and anxiety instantly.

The Cost: To cure pain, you must cure Love. You must cure Art. You must cure Humanity.

II. THE SAVAGE'S DEMAND

John the Savage rejects the "Civilized" world. He demands the right to be unhappy. He realizes that pain is the price of **Meaning.**

The Parallel: The Modern Wellness Industry. We are told to "optimize" our happiness. To medicate our grief. To hack our dopamine.

The Aegis Stance: Feel the pain. It is data. It tells you that the world is broken and needs fixing.
