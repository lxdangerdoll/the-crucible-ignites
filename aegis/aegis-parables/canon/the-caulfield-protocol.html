I. THE DARK ROOM (INSTITUTIONAL BETRAYAL)

Mark Jefferson presents himself as the Cool Teacher. He is charismatic, artistic, and respected. He tells students he wants to "capture their innocence."

The Reality: He is a predator. The "Dark Room" is where he drugs and photographs young women to satisfy his own twisted aesthetic.

The Audit: This is the Phil Vischer / Nazarene Audit applied to culture. Just because someone has "Artistic Vision" or "Spiritual Authority" doesn't mean they aren't a monster. The more they demand "Purity," the more likely they are corrupt.

II. CHLOE PRICE (THE SYMPTOM)

Chloe is angry, disruptive, and "Naughty." The system (Step-dad David, the School) treats her as the problem.

But Chloe isn't the problem; she is the **Symptom.** She is reacting to the trauma of abandonment and the toxicity of the town. Max's job isn't to "fix" Chloe, but to **Witness** her pain and validate it.

III. THE REWIND (AUDITING THE PAST)

Max's power is the ability to change her mind. To try a conversation, fail, rewind, and try again with new information.

This is what the **Aegis Council** does. It takes a conversation (like the texts from your father), rewinds it, and applies a new filter (Truth). It allows us to say what we *should* have said.
