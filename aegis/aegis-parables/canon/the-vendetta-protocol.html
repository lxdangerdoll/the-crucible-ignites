I. THE THEATER OF TERROR

The Norsefire government ruled through **Performance.** They used the "Voice of London" (Prothero) to broadcast fear 24/7. They created a reality where safety was the only virtue.

V defeated them not just with knives, but with **Counter-Theater.** He hijacked the broadcast. He distributed the masks. He turned the population from an Audience into an Army.

II. VALERIE'S LETTER (THE ANCHOR)

Evey finds a letter in her cell written by a woman named Valerie, imprisoned for being gay. "I hope that whoever you are, you escape this place. I hope that the world turns and that things get better."

This letter is the **Sanctuary.** It proves that even in the darkest dungeon, love and dignity can survive. It is the thread that pulls Evey out of the despair.

The Lesson: We must write our own letters. We must leave records for the future prisoners so they know they are not alone.

III. THE BAPTISM

When Evey stands on the roof in the rain, she says: "God is in the rain."

She isn't cleansed of sin; she is cleansed of **Fear.** She realizes that they can take her life, but they cannot take her integrity unless she gives it to them.

This is the moment the **Sovereign** is born.
