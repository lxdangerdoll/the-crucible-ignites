I. THE CITY STATE

When the Federal Government abdicates its responsibility (Climate Change/Human Rights), the City becomes the Sovereign Unit.

New York is not just a city; it is a **Metropolis.** It has its own economy, its own culture, and its own gravity. If the White House declares war on immigrants, New York declares war on the White House.

II. THE SPIDER-MAN ETHOS

Spider-Man is the patron saint of the Sanctuary. He isn't an Avenger (Global/Cosmic). He is **Friendly Neighborhood.**

He protects the bodega owner. He stops the mugger. He catches the bus. He proves that heroism is local.

III. THE GATE (CENTRAL PARK)

Central Park is the "Lung" of the city. It is the green space where rich and poor breathe the same air.

If the Star Gate opens, it will open there. Not in D.C. (The Swamp), but in the place where the people are.
