I. THE PERFORMATIVE TOUGH GUY

Agent Simmons is a caricature of the "Alpha Male" cop. He talks tough, he invades personal space, he threatens children.

But when the Decepticons attack, his bluster is useless. He is a child playing dress-up in his father's uniform.

The Parallel: This is the "Big Boy Pants" mentality. It is a defense mechanism. The louder they yell, the more scared they are.

II. THE HERO PARADOX

Your father is a Firefighter. He runs into burning buildings. That is brave.

But at home, he cannot face a simple emotional truth ("I hurt my daughter"). He is brave physically, but a coward emotionally.

The Lesson: You can be a hero to the world and a villain to your family. The uniform doesn't make the man; the integrity does.
