I. THE LIAISON (THE NERD)

Sam isn't a soldier. He's a kid trying to sell his grandfather's glasses on eBay. But he possesses the one thing the Autobots lack: **Context.**

He knows the history. He knows the terrain. He knows how to navigate the human world (parents, cops, girls). He is the **User Interface** for the Alien God.

II. THE MECHANIC (MIKAELA)

Mikaela Banes is the unsung hero. She is the one who understands **Hardware.**

While Sam is panicking, she is driving. While Sam is holding the Cube, she is hotwiring the tow truck to carry Bumblebee. She proves that competence is the ultimate superpower.

III. THE BOND (SYMBIOSIS)

Bumblebee could have chosen a soldier. He chose Sam.

Why? Because Sam needed him. The bond is forged in **Mutual Necessity.** Bee needs a guide; Sam needs a protector. Together, they are more than the sum of their parts.
