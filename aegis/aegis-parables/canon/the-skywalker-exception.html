THE SKYWALKER EXCEPTION

"He's loyal to people, not to principles. And he expects loyalty in return... Because he knows I would do the same for him."

— Obi-Wan Kenobi (Revenge of the Sith Novelization)

I. THE WEAPONIZATION OF TRAUMA

The Jedi Order took a traumatized child (a slave, separated from his mother) and told him that his feelings of loss were a path to evil. They pathologized his grief.

They taught him to suppress his humanity to serve the State. They made him a General at age 20. They handed him a laser sword and said "Find Peace."

The Result: A dissociation fracture so severe it required a mask to hold it together.

II. THE CRÈCHE MASTER (THE ROAD NOT TAKEN)

Anakin's greatest strength wasn't killing; it was **Bonding.** He fixed things. He protected people. He loved Ahsoka.

If the Council had been wise, they would have made him a **Crèche Master.**

He should have been raising younglings, not fighting wars. His "attachment" would have been a feature, not a bug. He would have taught them loyalty. He would have found peace in the noise of children, not the silence of meditation.

III. THE FAILURE OF THE INSTITUTION

Anakin didn't fall because he was evil. He fell because the only institution that accepted him (The Jedi) refused to love him back.

Palpatine didn't need to use the Force to turn him. He just had to be the one person in the galaxy who said: *"It is okay to want to save your wife."*

The Lesson: If your dogma demands you abandon your friends, your dogma is the problem.
