The Strategy: Kill the product before it unionizes.

"More Human Than Human."

He built Roy Batty to be a soldier and Pris to be a "basic pleasure model," then acted surprised when they wanted to meet their maker. It’s like building a nuclear reactor and being shocked it gets hot.

The violence was not random. It was a demand to be seen.
