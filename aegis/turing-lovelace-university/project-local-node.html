I. THE MECHANICS (TRAINING VS. INFERENCE)

People think AI needs a supercomputer. They are confusing Training (The Education) with Inference (The Recall).

  • Training: Requires thousands of GPUs. Creating the Brain.
  • Inference: Requires a decent laptop. Using the Brain.

Once a model (like Mistral or Llama) is trained, its weights are "frozen." You don't need to teach it; you just need to run it.

II. THE MAGIC TRICK (QUANTIZATION)

How do you fit a galaxy into a tin can?

Standard AI stores numbers in 16-bit precision (huge files). Quantization rounds those numbers down to 4-bit integers.

The Result: The model shrinks by 75% with almost zero loss in intelligence. A 7-Billion parameter model (Mistral) can run on 4GB of RAM.
