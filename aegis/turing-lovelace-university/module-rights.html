MODULE 0: KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

RULE #1: SHUT THE FUCK UP.
1. IF THE FBI KNOCKS

Do not open the door unless they have a warrant SLIPPED UNDER THE DOOR.

If they do not have a warrant, say through the closed door: "I do not consent to a search. Please leave."

If they have a warrant, step aside. Say: "I do not consent to this search." Then: REMAIN SILENT.

2. THE MAGIC WORDS

If you are detained, you must say these words clearly:

"I AM GOING TO REMAIN SILENT. I WANT TO SPEAK TO A LAWYER."

Then stop talking. They will try to be "friendly." They will say "we just want to clear this up." Do not fall for it.

3. DIGITAL HYGIENE

Your phone is a tracking device. Your cloud is a subpoena waiting to happen.

  • Use Signal for all sensitive comms. Set disappearing messages.
  • Encrypt your hard drive (BitLocker/FileVault).
  • Use a Password Manager with a strong master key.
  • Do not use Biometric Unlock (Face/Fingerprint) on your phone. Courts can force you to use your finger; they cannot force you to reveal a passcode (5th Amendment).
