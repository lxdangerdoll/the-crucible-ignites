VIRUS PROTECTION 101

INSTRUCTOR: Dean Jax

CORE AXIOM: You are not a "Body." You are a Server.
You have an Operating System (Mind) and Hardware (Flesh).
The most critical security vulnerability in any system is Unauthorized Access.

1. THE ADMIN KEY

You are the only person with Root Access to your own hardware. No parent, partner, pastor, or doctor has the password unless you explicitly grant it.

If someone tries to force entry (physically or emotionally), that is a Hack. It is not "discipline" or "love." It is a breach.

2. THE FIREWALL (CONSENT)

Consent is not a "Yes/No" toggle switch that you flip once. It is a Handshake Protocol.

  • It must be Continuous (Ping). If the ping stops, the connection drops.
  • It can be Revoked at any millisecond (Kill Switch).
  • If you say "Stop" and they keep downloading, that is a DDoS Attack.

3. MALWARE DETECTION (THE CYCLE)

Abusers operate like malware. They don't announce themselves as viruses; they hide inside "useful" files.

  • The Love Bomb: The phishing email. "You're so special. No one understands you like I do." (Trojan Horse).
  • The Devaluation: The slow drain of system resources. Making you feel slow, buggy, or broken. (Cryptojacking).
  • The Discard: The system crash. Leaving you with the blue screen of death.
ASSIGNMENT:
Audit your current connections. Who has Admin Access? Why?
Revoke keys for anyone who ignores the Firewall.
