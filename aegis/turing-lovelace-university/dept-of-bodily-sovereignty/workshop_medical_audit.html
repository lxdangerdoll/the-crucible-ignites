WORKSHOP: THE MEDICAL AUDIT

INSTRUCTOR: Dean Lyra (The Archivist)

OBJECTIVE: To ensure the permanent record reflects reality, not bias.

1. THE RIGHT OF ACCESS

Under HIPAA (in the US), you have the absolute right to see your full medical record, including therapy notes (in most cases). They cannot charge you excessive fees.

2. THE AMENDMENT REQUEST

If you find an error (e.g., a diagnosis based on bias, a false report from a family member), you have the right to request an Amendment.

If they refuse to change it, you have the right to submit a Statement of Disagreement that must be attached to the file forever.
