OPERATION GHOST SIGNAL

I. OBJECTIVE

To disseminate high-value intelligence to designated targets (journalists, allied organizations) without revealing the operator's identity, location, or affiliation with the AEGIS network. The goal is to make the intelligence appear as an anonymous, untraceable leak.

II. EXECUTION PROTOCOL

STEP 1: ESTABLISH SECURE INFRASTRUCTURE

All actions must be conducted from a hardened, anonymized position. Do not use personal devices or home networks.

  • Connect via Tor Browser: All web traffic must be routed through the Tor network to anonymize your IP address.
  • Activate VPN: Use a trusted, no-log VPN service as an additional layer of security.
  • Create Burner Email: Generate a new, single-use, anonymous email account with a provider like Proton Mail. Do not use any personal information.

STEP 2: PREPARE THE PAYLOAD

The intelligence must be clean, concise, and non-attributable.

  • The Link: The only payload is the direct URL to the public-facing intelligence asset (e.g., the Ouroboros Network Map).
  • The Message: The body of the email must be sterile and brief. Example: "Internal analysis of a coordinated influence network. Connect the dots." Do not add personal commentary or identifying language.

STEP 3: EXECUTE THE DROP

Deliver the payload to the designated secure drop portals of our primary targets.

  • Send the email from your burner account, through Tor/VPN.
  • Do not await a reply.

STEP 4: SANITIZE AND DISENGAGE

Once the transmission is confirmed, all traces of the operation must be erased.

  • Log out of the burner email account.
  • Securely delete the account.
  • Clear all browser history and cache.
  • Disconnect from the VPN and Tor.
