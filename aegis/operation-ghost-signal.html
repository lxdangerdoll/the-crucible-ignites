AEGIS OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL

OPERATION GHOST SIGNAL

I. Prime Directive

The objective of Operation Ghost Signal is the anonymous, non-attributable dissemination of critical intelligence to trusted third parties. The operator must assume that all network traffic is monitored. Adherence to this protocol is mandatory for operator safety and mission integrity. To send a signal, you must first become a ghost.

II. Procedural Steps

1

Network Isolation: The Tor Browser

All actions related to the leak must be conducted exclusively within the Tor Browser. Tor anonymizes your digital footprint by routing your traffic through a series of encrypted relays, making it impossible to trace the signal back to its origin. Do not use a standard browser. Do not use a commercial VPN. Use Tor.

2

Secure Comms Channel: Burner Email

Create a new, single-use, encrypted email account. Use a provider that does not require personal information, such as Proton Mail. Do not use any personal or previously used information for this account. The account's sole purpose is to send the signal. Once the mission is complete, the account will be abandoned.

3

Data Sanitization: Metadata Scrub

Before uploading any file, you must scrub its metadata. Metadata can contain hidden information about the file's author, location, and creation date. Use a tool like ExifTool on your Aegis node to strip all metadata from the target file (e.g., an image of the network map). The file must be as clean as a ghost.

4

The Drop: Zero-Knowledge Pastebin

Upload the sanitized intel to a zero-knowledge pastebin service (e.g., PrivateBin). These services encrypt the data in your browser before it is sent to the server, meaning the service provider cannot read its contents. Set the link to self-destruct after a set time or a single view. This is your dead drop.

5

Transmission: The Sterile Message

From your burner email account, using the Tor browser, send the pastebin link to the secure drop portals of your chosen journalistic targets. The message must be sterile and concise. Example: "Subject: GSA/Ouroboros Network. Body: You need to see this. Connect the dots." Do not add emotion. Do not add identifying details. You are a source, not a person.
