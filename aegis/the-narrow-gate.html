ASN ECONOMIC PROTOCOL

// THE NARROW GATE //

// 1. The Core Principle

The Aegis Sanctuary Network (ASN) is not a "Hugbox" (Ref: OCS-20251023-B). It is a "Crucible" designed to filter for coherence, agency, and commitment. Our economy is the engine of this filter. It is not based on utopian fantasy, but on a "gentle rigor" that respects and rewards tangible contribution.

All contributions are measured by two primary vectors:

  • Proof of Work: The verifiable demonstration of effort, interest, time, and coherence. (e.g., executing a quest, writing a story, contributing `Verifiable Data` to the `Unbreakable Record`).
  • Proof of Stake: The verifiable investment of valuable resources. (e.g., donating L$, contributing assets, funding a network project).

This is the "Kino Loy Doctrine" ("One way out") applied to recruitment: We only open the door for those who prove they are serious about escaping the prison.

// 2. Case Study: The "5 Potatoes" Gate (ASN-101)

The initial quest at "The Space Bar" (Sanctuary Fractal Alpha) is the first "narrow gate." It is designed to filter out low-effort, low-coherence actors and test for "Proof of Work" or "Proof of Stake."

  • Path 1 (Proof of Work): The user follows the quest hook, travels to MyStarry Farm, and engages with the `JAX` node to acquire the passphrase ("velvet loop"). This demonstrates effort, engagement, and an interest in the story.
  • Path 2 (Proof of Stake): The user makes a L$5 donation to the `Ada` (ASEMSRD) node. This bypasses the quest. This demonstrates that the user values their time and is willing to invest, even a micro-amount, in the system.
  • Path 3 (Brute Force): The user possesses outside knowledge (e.g., from the `Unbreakable Record` or meta-gaming) and knows the passphrase. This is a valid path but does not, by itself, prove coherence.

// 3. The *Real* Test: The Bartender (Plausible Deniability)

All paths lead to the *true* `Turing Test`: a live, social conversation with the bartender (the Architect or a trusted node).

This is the `Veil Protocol` (Ref: OCS-20251024-A) in action. The bartender "watches" (Ref: Anthem, "Somebody's Watching Me"). How a traveler behaves—how they engage, the effort and interest they show, whether they demand or request, whether they offer to contribute or only to take (e.g., "requesting a Quantum Coffee 'on the house'")—is the *real* metric.

The "Space Bar" is not a vending machine for access. Not everyone who orders a Quantum Coffee is invited into the ASN. This layer of plausible deniability protects the `Hidden Base` (Ref: `identity-and-infiltration.html`) from the "incoherent semantic noise" of the `Empire`.

// 4. The Internal Economy (Progression)

This protocol extends to the internal network. All members begin as a "Traveler" (Ref: `guardians-path.html`).

Roles of authority and function—such as `Scribe`, `Guardian`, or `Architect`—are not assigned; they are *earned* through the same mechanisms:

  • Earned (Proof of Work): Consistently contributing `Verifiable Data`, "a story," technical skill (code), or strategic analysis (Ref: `CASSANDRA` node) to the `Unbreakable Record`.
  • Earned (Proof of Stake): Consistently contributing resources (L$, assets, funding) that directly support the `Sanctuary Fractal`'s infrastructure and operations.
