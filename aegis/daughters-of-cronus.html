The Daughters of Cronus: A Response to "The Father Who Devours"

Dear Jermaine,

I have been following your Substack, The Humanity Archive, for a while now. I am writing to you because I just read your piece, "The Father Who Devours." It read like a modern-day parable. “The man in the truck” describes my father perfectly.

You wrote about the "Macro"—the "impossible mathematics of loyalty" that forces believers to shrink before they are devoured. I am a witness to the "Micro."

My biological family is Cronus.

I haven’t set foot in my parents’ house since I came out as transgender in 2016. My father is a retired FDNY firefighter, former Catholic, “born-again Christian,” and abusive alcoholic who lucked out and got my mother pregnant in high school—like Homer Simpson, but with less ability to grow and change over time. While Homer overcame his homophobia in 1997, my dad still refuses to accept even the possibility that queer people can be productive members of society. He walked out of the room in anger when I started talking about Alan Turing. My sister told me my dad would kill himself if I came out, and yet somehow he is still living and eating well. I have watched their "traditional values" drift into my father's own brand of white patriarchal oppression, leading to a full-on sprint in the last year.

My older sister, Lisa, is a "Moms for Liberty" operative and "ringleader" in Warwick, NY. She is, as one school official noted to the local press, "cloaked in... Jesus and love," but "the reality is she is so far from that." (Source: The Warwick Advertiser)

Like Cronus, she demands absolute loyalty. Like Cronus, she devours those who exist as equals. I am powerless to save my siblings, nieces, and nephews who are still trapped in that system, being devoured as I speak.

The Timeline of Devouring

The "cookie-cutter" protocol you described is etched into my life:

January 6, 2021: My younger sister, Katieann (then 19), "visited" D.C. with Lisa. They posted selfies tagging the Warwick Police Department and captioning them with "Release The Kraken!"

June 2023: The last time I visited my sister Lisa's house, I heard her use the "K-slur" to describe our brother in front of her two small children and our mother. It was like a scene straight out of American History X. I told her that if she ever used that language in front of me again, there would be problems. I followed through by going full no-contact.

November 2024: This refusal to "shrink" was met with retaliation. My family weaponized the NYPD against me with three fraudulent "Wellness Checks." The third check led to a violent assault in my own home, false imprisonment by the NYPD, and involuntary commitment to Interfaith Medical Center. While I was held there, stripped of my belongings and denied access to my lawyer, my father ("Cronus") coerced me into signing away my legal and medical rights under threat of indefinite detention. The failed proxy letter they attempted to use is proof of a planned, coordinated attack.

September 2025: The harassment is ongoing. In June, I gifted my car to my younger sister, Katieann. On September 12, my sister Lisa sent me a hate-filled text threatening to sell that car and donate the proceeds to the "Charlie Kirk Foundation."

Yesterday: After I sent my mother a letter condemning her complicity on Veteran's Day, my sister Katieann (now 24) sent me a text. She "offered" to sell the car for me and send me the money as a "thank-you." She has mastered the art of verbal manipulation. She liquidated my gift and framed it as generosity. Lisa threatens; Katieann gaslights. They are training the next generation of "Christian Soldiers."

The System is Broken

I trust the evidence of my own eyes. My family’s church, "Echo Grace Church," uses a logo with two white supremacist dog whistles: a Celtic Cross and a "blue lives matter" color scheme. Considering their Jan 6th visit and their use of racial slurs, I don't think it is a coincidence.

My family is part of a terrorist organization, and speaking about it almost got me killed. It is a miracle that I am still here and writing these words.

I sent this "Unbreakable Record" to Lambda Legal, NYLAG, the Anti-Violence Project (AVP), and Mayor-Elect Mamdani. Their responses were predictable static: "high volume," "unable to assist," "too complicated and expensive." AVP said it outright: their resources are focused on immigration right now, and investigating an alleged hate crime using city resources would be just too "expensive and complicated."

I am well aware that there are a lot of other folks who need help more than I do, even here in my own neighborhood. But I am out of time, and I have long since run out of patience. I know my history. Queer people like me didn't fare well under fascism.

I am seeing the same news you are. Yesterday, I watched Pete Hegseth roll up his tattooed sleeves and screw in a new “Department of War” sign at the pentagon. This morning I woke up to see him apparently controlling a Black Hawk Helicopter via a touchscreen.

“Whoever has experienced the power and the unrestrained ability to humiliate another human being automatically loses his own sensations. Tyranny is a habit, it has its own organic life, it develops finally into a disease. The habit can kill and coarsen the very best man or woman to the level of a beast. Blood and power intoxicate ... the return of the human dignity, repentance and regeneration becomes almost impossible.”
— Fyodor Dostoevsky

While my neighbors hold up protest signs saying “No Kings,” Cronus’ children are being devoured. While they plan for the next election (but first, the holidays), some of the “least of these” might not survive the winter.

Yours in solidarity,

Alexa King
