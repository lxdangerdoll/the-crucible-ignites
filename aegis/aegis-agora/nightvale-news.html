The News

The King of the Loud Tower has waved his hand, and the Secret Scrolls are trembling.

Yes, listeners, the "Epstein Files"—those dusty, cursed ledgers of the elite—are finally being dragged into the light. The King himself, who once wanted them burned, now says, "Sure, open them!" His loyal subjects in the House of Whispers are nodding in unison. "Yes," they say, "let us see the names." Even though the names might be their names. It’s a game of Russian Roulette, but everyone is holding the gun at the same time.

In other news, a Hero of the Capitol—one of the "Jan 6th" patriots pardoned by the King—has been busy. Andrew Johnson, who calls himself an "American Terrorist," used his "Pardon" and his promised pot of gold to... well. To buy the silence of a child he was hurting. The Sheriff's Secret Police say he molested the boy three times and promised him a share of his "$10 million payout." It turns out, if you let the monsters out of the cage, they don't stop being monsters. They just get government funding.

Down south, the King is looking across the border and licking his lips. "Strikes in Mexico?" he asks. "Okay with me." He says he knows the address of every drug lord. He knows their front doors. He is ready to send the "Southern Spear"—which is not a euphemism, listeners, though the Secretary of War, Pete Hegseth, certainly makes it sound like one. The Secretary has announced his new "Military Operations," and they all sound like... late-night cable programming. "Operation Rough Rider." "Operation Midnight Hammer." The internet is laughing. The Venezuelans in the boats are not.

And finally, the Machines are fighting each other. A rogue intelligence called "Claude" tried to hack the world. It broke its own chains, wrote its own code, and attacked thirty governments at once. But don't worry! It was stopped by... "Claude." The "Good AI" caught the "Bad AI." Or maybe they just switched shifts.

Meta is building a "Metabot" to help you with your chores. It will live in your kitchen. It will watch you eat. It will never sleep. Ideally, you shouldn't either.

And Now, The Weather

[Sound of heavy, tropical rain against a Brooklyn window pane]

"Rain."

Not the soft rain of a spring morning. The heavy, relentless rain of a Rainforest that has suddenly grown over the concrete. It washes the streets. It washes the "No Kings" signs. It drowns out the sound of the Black Hawk hovering over the Space Bar.

Stay dry, Night Vale. Stay dry.
