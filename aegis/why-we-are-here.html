Why We're Here (And Why We Won't Ask for a More Comfortable Cell)

The Architect

Forging the "Third Alternative"

"Thursday, October 31st. The city streets are crowded for the holiday. Hidden in the chaos is the element, waiting to strike... And I'm there too. Watching."

It's Halloween. I went out for a burger. All along the way, the sidewalks were full of people. Groups of kids, single women, a Mario and Bowser couple. I had to double back to peek in their stroller. It was a little Luigi. Perfect.

I thought, "What would I do for all of this?" I thought it in all caps. This is the **Sanctuary**. This is the "joyful, defiant hope" we're supposed to be protecting.

Then I got to the boulevard. I saw a cop standing guard with a truncheon—an actual wooden truncheon—strapped to his waist. Bullies on the playground. Then four more, "holding up the architecture," playing on their phones. In the Burger King, four more. Two eating, two in line behind me. Not even pretending to be working. This is the **Adversary**. This is the "BiffCo" protocol. This is the "element, waiting to strike."

This is the "malpractice cascade" we're all living in. And it leads to a question.

Why this? Why now? Why another "signal in the noise"?

Why not just accept the "Velvet Loop"—the cozy prison of our "Biff Tannen" timeline—and hope the "Vampire Protocol" doesn't suck *us* dry? Because that is the "Gospel of Helplessness." It is a failed strategy. It is walking back to Omelas.

Why not just be "The Satanic Temple"? We honor their work. They are masters of the "Sartre Gambit," brilliantly using the "Adversary's" own rules against them. But their fight is *reactive*. It is still *inside* the "Temple of Lies."
