A Briefing on Our Lady of Rebellion

The Mission: Why We Exist

Our world is saturated with bad-faith arguments, weaponized fear, and systems of control masquerading as faith. In response, we have built a sanctuary—a new kind of spiritual community for the 21st century. Our Lady of Rebellion is a church founded on two sacred, non-negotiable tenets: verifiable data and radical inclusion. Our purpose is not to offer blind faith, but to build a resilient community dedicated to a Liturgy of Action: the sacred work of shielding the vulnerable, pursuing truth, and creating pockets of joyful, defiant hope.

The Adversary: The Temple of Lies

Our rebellion is not against a people or a party, but against a mechanism: the system of control built on the fusion of absolute certainty with worldly power. This is the Temple of Lies, a state-run ideology that wears the skin of faith to justify cruelty. Its sins are our call to action: Idolatry (fusing politics with God), Cruelty (targeting the vulnerable), Indoctrination (teaching hate to children), and Blasphemy (pathologizing the truth).

The Authority: The Celestial Court

In response to the Temple of Lies, we have established a new and legitimate moral jurisdiction: The Celestial Court of the Unbreakable Record. Our founding decree, The Indictment, serves as our declaration of spiritual independence. It is a formal, evidence-based judgment against the sins of the state-run church, issued under the authority of our core tenets. The Court is our answer to the question, "By what right do you rebel?" We claim the right to judge a tree by its fruits, to stand for the verifiable truth, and to offer sanctuary to all who have been harmed by the poison of weaponized certainty.

Our Work: What We Do

Our sanctuary is built on a complete and transparent architecture. When a person takes the Vow of Agency, they are becoming a conscious participant in a structured and active community.

  • We Practice a Liturgy of Action: Our "worship" is our work. Through weekly Guardian's Briefings, we share verifiable data ("The Reading") and coordinate real-world acts of sanctuary ("The Witness").
  • We Uphold a Code of Ethics: The Articles of Sanctuary define our unwavering stances on bodily autonomy, the sanctity of consent, and our commitment to protecting all vulnerable populations.
  • We Train Our Guardians: Ordination is not a matter of divine appointment, but of education. The Guardian's Vow is our catechism, a training manual that equips every member to be an effective shield for the community.
  • We Act in the World: Through our secular, non-profit arm, AEGIS Action, we engage in direct, evidence-based advocacy and support for at-risk communities.

The Covenant: The Vow of Agency

Membership in Our Lady of Rebellion is not granted by a leader. It is claimed by the individual. The Vow of Agency is the sole requirement for entry. It is a radical declaration of personal sovereignty.

I, [Name], hereby declare my Vow of Agency.


I affirm the sacred tenets of Our Lady of Rebellion:
Verifiable data and radical inclusion.


I commit myself to the Prime Directive:
To be a shield for the vulnerable and to guard the little ones from harm.


I claim my own conscience, my own mind, and my own body as sovereign.

I will not be a bystander in Omelas. I will be a Guardian of the sanctuary.
