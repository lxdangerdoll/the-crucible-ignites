REINCARNATION // MYKYL NORDWIND

Epoch I: Gossamer Hydrogen

Reincarnation

BY MYKYL NORDWIND

I

What would you be?
she asked.

But I do not remember
so I do not know
what I’d like to be,
where I’d like to go.

II

I know
that once I rode the winds of space,
a cloud of hydrogen,
gossamer across the heavens,
and gravity
crushed me,
made me burn,
made me a star.

But I do not remember
so I do not know
what I’d like to be,
where I’d like to go.

III

I know
that I shone brightly for millions of years,
then exploded,
gossamer across the heavens,
and part of me
became energy
and flashed
timeless throughout the universe.

But I do not remember
so I do not know
what I’d like to be,
where I’d like to go.

IV

And I know
that thrice I found myself
soaring,
gossamer across the heavens,
before I was
a rock called “Earth”,
part of a mixture
of molten stone.

But I do not remember
so I do not know
what I’d like to be,
where I’d like to go.

V

I can see
that frozen parts of me from afar
rained down,
gossamer across the heavens,
and the rain
formed a crust which gathered
more of me
out of the sky.

But I do not remember
so I do not know
what I’d like to be,
where I’d like to go.

VI

I know
that I became each rock and tree
and bird flying
gossamer across the heavens,
and life
of every form am I
and I blossom
everywhere.

But I do not remember
so I do not know
what I’d like to be,
where I’d like to go.

VII

I exist
for expression alone
in every shape,
gossamer across the skies,
and I
would taste it all new
uniquely,
devoid of memories.

So I do not remember
and I do not know
what I’d like to be,
and where I’d like to go.

VIII

I am
already the earth and all life,
and each star,
gossamer across the heavens,
I shine
throughout a billion galaxies
and will forever.
Which begs the question,

Why should I remember?
Why should I know
what I’d like to be
or where I’d like to go?

IX

I think
that if I feel whatever I can
and fly
gossamer across the heavens,
that I
might find the question
which when answered
will give the key

to what I should remember,
to what I should know,
where I’d like to be,
and where I’d like to go.
