SHEDDING THE ROBES // MYKYL NORDWIND

Epoch I: The Falling Self

Shedding the Robes

BY MYKYL NORDWIND // JUNE 13, 2007

I

The robes of self
fall to the ground
and she stands
naked before the wind
arms outstretched to the sky
caressed
by the sun and the moon
and the dreams
of everyone
everywhere
everywhen.

II

Her body,
touched by the earth
tasted by the sky
and consumed by the stars,
fades
and joins with her mother,
the universe.

III

Only then,
when individuality has fled
does she become
that which she could
that which dreams
that which survives
that which understands
eternity.

IV

Reality is
a fantasy
created by selves
defined by individuals -
a mountain built
of illusion -
and to leap
from the precipice of self
is to reach
the truth
the unity
the beauty
of all that is
and all
that can be.
